Import from Letterboxd — Later

→ →

Later can import your Letterboxd watchlist, watched films, ratings, and reviews from Letterboxd’s official data export.

Later is not affiliated with Letterboxd.

How to import

  1. On a computer or phone browser, go to letterboxd.com and sign in.
  2. Open Settings → Import & Export (or go to the data export page).
  3. Export your data and download the ZIP file. (Export may require Letterboxd Pro.)
  4. On iPhone or iPad, open Later → Settings → Import.
  5. Choose the Letterboxd ZIP (or the CSV files inside). Wait while Later matches titles via TMDB.

Notes

  • Movies only — TV shows in the export are not imported.
  • Unmatched titles are skipped if Later cannot find them on TMDB.
  • Import merges with your existing Later library (it does not wipe it).
  • Your library data stays on your device.
